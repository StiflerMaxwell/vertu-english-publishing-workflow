# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment.
Be constructive, assume good intent, discuss the work rather than the person,
and respect privacy and confidentiality boundaries.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private data or credentials, and sustained
disruption of project collaboration.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments or issues that violate
these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone
involved.
